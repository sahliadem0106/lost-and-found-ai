Lost & Found

A six-year-old disappears in a busy mall. From nothing but the parents' description, an agent follows the family across the security cameras and finds where the boy was lost and where he is now. A camera map decides where to look, CreativAI watches only that footage, and the agent joins the dots. Everything below is a replay of a real run, including every prompt sent to CreativAI and every answer it gave.
