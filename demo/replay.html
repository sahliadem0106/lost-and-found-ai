The Map

Where could they be?

Our own code, no AI. It knows the 12 cameras, the walkways between them in metres, and that adults walk 0.6–1.8 m/s and children 0.4–1.6 m/s. From one sighting it works out which cameras are reachable next and in which minutes. Footage outside those windows is never looked at.

The Eyes · CreativAI

What is in this clip?

For the clips the Map picked, CreativAI builds a data plate and answers three questions per clip: is the family here, is the child with them or alone, and at what second does the child walk away? The answers are cached, so a replay costs nothing.

The agent

Join the dots

It follows the family in time order until the child walks away, then follows the child, counting only sightings where the child is alone. Lookalikes with adults are ruled out. Every move is checked against walking speed. Then it alerts the nearest guard.
